About MineOS AI

Problem Statement 26009 · SIH 2026

Vision

MineOS AI integrates satellite imagery, geological intelligence, production history, ERP data, equipment health, weather information, and operational activity into one platform capable of making proactive mining decisions — for MOIL Ltd.

From dashboard to decision engine

Current workflow: Data → Dashboard → Human Decision

MineOS workflow: Data → AI → Decision Engine → Recommendation → Human Approval

Research foundation

Built on 9 research papers, MOIL's existing digital systems (QR asset management, SAP ERP, underground activity digitization), the SIH problem statement, and space technology inputs. Current research solves reserve prediction, production forecasting, maintenance prediction, and fleet scheduling independently — no existing work integrates all four into one decision layer.

Guiding principle

Every feature must solve a problem in the SIH statement, address a research gap, extend MOIL's existing digital ecosystem, or provide measurable operational value. Features satisfying all four get top priority.
